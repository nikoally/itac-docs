# Introduction

Welcome to the Southern New England ITAC documentation site.

This site is a technical resource for industrial energy assessments. It brings together training material, report guidance, calculation tools, and documented methods for identifying and evaluating energy-efficiency opportunities.

The University of Connecticut Industrial Training and Assessment Center (UC-ITAC) provides free energy, productivity, and waste assessments to small and medium-sized industrial facilities through funding from the United States Department of Energy. Learn more in the [UC ITAC Impact Overview](https://itac.university/center/university-of-connecticut).

# Explore

- **Training:** foundational material on energy, engineering economics, technical writing, and industrial systems.

- **Recommendations:** calculation methods for common energy-efficiency measures.

- **Report Guidance:** practical guidance for facility assessments and technical reports.

- **Calculators:** interactive tools for selected savings calculations.

- **[ITAC official website](https://itac.university/):** program information, assessment protocols, ARC codes, and other resources.

# Access and Contributions

Security and confidentiality are essential. Before proposing content, ensure that all facility and client information is fully anonymized.

Instructions for proposing corrections and additions are available in the [ITAC documentation repository](https://github.com/UCONN-ITAC/itac-docs).
